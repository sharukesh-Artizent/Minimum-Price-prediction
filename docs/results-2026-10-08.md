## Findings

### 1. Success Criteria Analysis

- **F1 (Decision agreement 67.0% vs >= 80% threshold — FAIL):**
  The inferred baseline B0 captures Assurant's aggregate floor distribution well (F2 revenue agreement passes within 4.0%), but individual offer-level agreement is 67.0%. Historical decisions exhibit trader discretion, buyer-specific concessions, and unobserved attributes not captured by the point-in-time features.
- **F2 (B0 vs ACTUAL revenue agreement +4.0% vs +/-5% — PASS):**
  Replay revenue under B0 ($2.853B) tracks ACTUAL revenue ($2.743B) within 4.0%, validating that the interval-censored AFT model provides an accurate reflection of historical aggregate monetization.
- **F3 (ACTUAL x recall vs matched-sale revenue +52.1% vs +/-10% — FAIL):**
  The unweighted match recall is ~46.5%, but sales linkage is non-uniform across ticket size: high-value, fast-moving items match at substantially higher rates than long-tail SKUs, creating a divergence between transaction-count recall and dollar-volume realization.
- **S1 (P2 - B0 total value uplift in Segment A — PASS):**
  Segment A total value uplift 95% bootstrap CI lower bound is +$77.7M (overall point estimate +$111.7M, +3.5%), confirming robust, statistically significant value creation on the core 80% volume SKUs.
- **S2 (Sell-through drop -43.2 points vs >= -2.0 points — FAIL):**
  P2 trades volume for price realization: sell-through declines from 89.8% to 46.7%, while price realization rises from 98.3% to 105.7%. Direct acceptance drops from 50.3% to 7.4%, shifting volume into counters (51.4%) and rejections (41.2%). This occurs because the estimated holding cost ($0.1\%$/day) and low estimated depreciation ($\delta \approx 0.045\%$/day) imply a very high continuation value $W$, making holding for future offers economically attractive under the model.
- **S3 (M1 market value MAPE — FAIL in single, PASS in walk-forward):**
  In the single-window test, LightGBM MAPE (0.045) was slightly above naive (0.042) due to regime changes over the 6-week horizon. In walk-forward retraining, LightGBM outperformed naive (0.041 vs 0.042), satisfying S3.
- **S4 (Uplift sign stability under sensitivity — PASS):**
  P2 total value uplift over B0 remains positive ($+\$111.6\text{M}$ to $+\$111.9\text{M}$, +3.5%) across all four sensitivity permutations of holding cost ($h \times \{0.5, 1.5\}$) and depreciation ($\delta \times \{0.5, 1.5\}$).

### 2. Walk-Forward vs Single-Window Consistency

The backtest results demonstrate remarkable consistency between single-window and weekly walk-forward evaluation:
- **P2 vs B0 Total Value Uplift:** Single window = +$111.7M (+3.5%), Walk-forward = +$111.0M (+3.5%).
- **P2 Price Realization:** Single window = 105.7%, Walk-forward = 105.6%.
- **P2 Sell-Through:** Single window = 46.7%, Walk-forward = 47.3%.
- **ORACLE Ceiling:** Single window = +$87.7M over P2, Walk-forward = +$91.6M over P2.

### 3. Assumptions Still Pending Confirmation with Assurant

1. **Holding cost $h = 0.1\%$ per day ($36.5\%$ annualized):** As demonstrated by the frontier analysis, higher holding costs encourage lower reserves and higher sell-through. Calibrating true warehouse carrying costs with Assurant finance is critical.
2. **Salvage floor = 50% of market value:** Serves as a hard backstop; requires operational validation.
3. **Grade ladder monotonicity:** Enforced strictly across standard grades (D < C < C+ < B < B+ < A < A+), leaving non-standard grades (REC, R, L, UNK) unconstrained.
4. **Counter take-up and offer conversion:** Replay assumes buyers respond to counter offers per the calibrated isotonic curve and that all accepted offers convert to executed orders.
